<rationale>  
The revised strategy addresses core weaknesses identified in evaluations: (1) Previous implementations failed to distinguish between repetitive vs. unique details, causing wasted token space. Structured encoding resolves this by explicitly tagging novel elements (e.g., "Submerged vehicles" instead of repeating "flooded"). (2) Contradictions emerged due to stale/deleted evidence – automated validation now flags mismatches (e.g., rejecting "No vehicles" despite explicit image confirmation). (3) Token constraints led to arbitrary deletions; the scoring-based retention policy ensures critical incidents dominate memory. Question-specific injection improved relevance recall, reducing abstentions where prior attempts misapplied generic summaries. These changes align with evaluation outcomes showing higher correctness rates when distinctive facts persist.  
</rationale>
