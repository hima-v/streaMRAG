Context tags bridge disparate events (e.g., linking railway damage across flood/wildfire streams), addressing the observed abstentions due to missing contextual connections. The stricter eviction policy ensures newer, more complex scenarios remain accessible despite earlier simple descriptions. This aligns with evaluation metrics showing better performance correlates with preserving technical details like "four ladders" vs generic "emergency equipment".
